Why does ½ = ²⁄₄ = ⁴⁄₈?

Watch how the pieces change while the shaded amount stays the same.

Read the explanation

Shade one of two equal parts: one half. Split every part in two. Two quarters cover the same amount as one half. Split again: four eighths cover that same amount. Multiplying the numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number gives an equivalent fraction.
