How high does the ladder reach?

Use sine, cosine and tangent to connect an angle to real distances.

Read the explanation

A 5 m ladder makes a 37° angle with level ground. The wall and ground form a right angle. Relative to the ground angle, the height is the opposite side, the ground distance is the adjacent side, and the ladder is the hypotenuse.

sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse; cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse; tan θ = opposite / adjacent. So height = 5 sin 37° ≈ 3.01 m, and ground distance = 5 cos 37° ≈ 3.99 m. Use degrees for these calculations. The drawing uses a 3–4–5 triangle, whose ground angle is approximately 36.87°; the calculations use the rounded 37°.
